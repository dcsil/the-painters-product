# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The pAInters team is committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone — regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and professionally in all project spaces
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Acknowledge and credit others' contributions

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Disrespectful, dismissive, or harassing communication
- Personal attacks or derogatory comments
- Deliberate intimidation or exclusion
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any external testing or communication channels associated with this project.

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the team via a private message to any team member. The team will review the report collectively and take appropriate action, which may include a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from the project. Unresolved disputes may be escalated to course staff (CSC491, University of Toronto).

All reports will be handled confidentially.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from community standards established across open source projects and reflects the norms expected in academic software engineering courses at the University of Toronto.
