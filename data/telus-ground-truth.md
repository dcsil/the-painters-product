# TELUS Telecom — Ground Truth Reference

## Company Overview
- TELUS is one of Canada's largest telecommunications companies, headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia.
- TELUS operates nationally across Canada, providing wireless, internet, TV, and home security services.
- TELUS is publicly traded on the TSX (T) and NYSE (TU).

## Wireless Plans (Approximate — Subject to Change)
- TELUS wireless plans vary by province and are updated frequently. Exact pricing should always be verified on telus.com.
- Typical plan ranges: basic plans start around $50-60/month, mid-tier $70-85/month, premium plans $90+/month.
- Data allotments vary: common tiers include 20GB, 50GB, 100GB, and unlimited options.
- Family/multi-line discounts are available but vary.
- TELUS does NOT guarantee specific prices indefinitely — promotional pricing has end dates.

## Network
- TELUS operates on a national 4G LTE and 5G network.
- 5G coverage is available in major Canadian cities but is not nationwide.
- TELUS shares network infrastructure with Bell Canada in many areas through a network sharing agreement.
- Network uptime claims should not be stated as exact percentages (e.g., "99.99%") unless citing a specific published SLA.

## Customer Service Policies
- TELUS offers a 15-day Buyer's Remorse period for new device purchases/activations.
- Early cancellation fees apply to device financing agreements (not service contracts since CRTC Wireless Code changes).
- TELUS participates in the Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) for dispute resolution.

## Common Misstatements to Watch For
- Citing specific J.D. Power rankings or awards without verifiable dates and categories.
- Quoting exact plan prices that may be outdated or region-specific.
- Claiming TELUS has won "most awards" or "best network" without specifying the source, year, and metric.
- Stating exact customer satisfaction percentages without citing the specific survey.
- Guaranteeing specific network speeds — actual speeds depend on location, congestion, and device.
- Quoting exact infrastructure investment dollar amounts without citing the annual report year.
- Claiming specific uptime guarantees for consumer services (SLAs are typically for enterprise).

## TELUS Sub-Brands
- **Koodo Mobile**: TELUS's flanker brand offering prepaid and postpaid plans at lower price points.
- **Public Mobile**: TELUS's budget brand offering self-serve prepaid plans.
- Both use the TELUS network but have different plan structures and pricing.

## Regulatory Context
- Canadian wireless is regulated by the CRTC (Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission).
- The CRTC Wireless Code governs contract terms, unlocking, and consumer rights.
- Number portability is mandated — customers can transfer numbers between carriers.
